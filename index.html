COSMIWAY

Shaunak Okhde

Founder · Builder · Architect

Cosmiway Labs is built around the idea of creating useful digital tools and bringing them together into a connected ecosystem. Shaunak Okhde founded Cosmiway Labs and continues to shape its projects, direction and growing collection of tools.

What started as individual projects continues to expand into a larger universe of digital experiences.
