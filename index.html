About the founder

The person
behind it.

Cosmiway isn't a collection that appeared out of nowhere. It is the result of one person continually turning ideas into things that can actually be used.

Founder

Shaunak
Okhde

Founder · Builder · Architect

Shaunak Okhde is the founder and builder behind Cosmiway Labs. The ecosystem grew from the idea of creating useful digital tools and giving each of them its own purpose and identity.

Instead of treating every project as a completely separate thing, Cosmiway brings them together into one larger universe. AI, communication, accounts, mail, meetings, storage and notes can all become parts of that growing collection.

The projects are built iteratively. An idea becomes a prototype, the prototype becomes a real tool, and the tools gradually become an ecosystem.
