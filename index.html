Getting started

Start exploring.

First time here?

  1. Go to SτAU ID.
  2. Click Create ID.
  3. Enter your email.
  4. Create a password.
  5. Choose your username.
  6. Click Create.
  7. Return to the portal and explore the tools.

Already have an ID?

  1. Enter your email ID.
  2. Enter your password.
  3. Sign in.
  4. You're ready to go.

Shaunak Okhde is a solo developer and builder who likes turning ideas into working things.


His work goes beyond SτAU itself: he spends his time building software, experimenting with technology, exploring ideas and creating projects across different areas of interest.


He approaches projects as both a builder and an architect — figuring out how something should work, then actually putting it together. SτAU is one expression of that process, rather than the entirety of what he does.
