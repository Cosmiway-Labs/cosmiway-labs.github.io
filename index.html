First time here?

Start in minutes.

Getting into the Cosmiway ecosystem is straightforward. Create your Cosmic Login once, then use it to get started.

Create your Cosmic ID

  1. 1 Go to Cosmic Login.
  2. 2 Click Create ID.
  3. 3 Enter your email address.
  4. 4 Create your password.
  5. 5 Choose your username.
  6. 6 Click Create.
  7. 7 Return to the portal and explore the tools.

Already have an account?

  1. 1 Open Cosmic Login.
  2. 2 Enter your email ID.
  3. 3 Enter your password.
  4. 4 Sign in and you're ready to go.
About the founder

The person behind the build.

SO

Shaunak Okhde

Founder · Builder · Architect

Cosmiway Labs is driven by a simple idea: digital tools should feel useful instead of unnecessarily complicated. Shaunak Okhde founded Cosmiway Labs to build that kind of ecosystem — a growing collection of tools designed around practical use, experimentation and continuous improvement.

The projects continue to evolve as new ideas become tools, and tools become part of a larger connected ecosystem.
